## 1. The Core Scientific Imperative

In computational oncology and AI-assisted biology, **the greatest risk is not lack of data, but ungrounded extrapolation (hallucination)**. Many AI platforms make claims about molecular mechanisms based merely on co-occurrence in literature or vague visual patterns.

BioLayers AI enforces a strict three-tier evidence classification on every edge and node in the platform:
1. **□ Observed directly in image (`observed_directly_in_image`):**
   - Quantitative measurement derived directly from calibrated pixel/voxel data with verified physical units (µm, µm², µm/min, fluorophore intensity AU, pixel counts).
2. **▤ Supported by literature (`supported_by_literature`):**
   - Peer-reviewed published experimental finding with specific PubMed ID (PMID), DOI, and defined experimental scope (e.g., Western blot, qPCR, ELISA, knockout mouse model).
3. **◇ Inferred by model (`inferred_by_model`):**
   - Computational hypothesis or statistical prediction generated by an AI model (e.g. segmentation boundary, tracking link probability, graph embedding link); **must be visually flagged with amber badges and never presented as verified empirical fact**.

## 2. Boundary Matrix: What Microscopy CAN vs CANNOT Prove

| Feature / Question | Can Bioimage Data Prove This? | Required Orthogonal Evidence for Verification |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Cell motility velocity (µm/min)** | **YES** (Live time-lapse) | Calibrated spatial scale (µm/px) and temporal interval (min/frame). |
| **Directional persistence ratio** | **YES** (Live time-lapse) | Continuous track coordinates \((x_t, y_t)\) across at least 15+ consecutive frames. |
| **Nuclear morphometry & compaction** | **YES** (Fluorescence/Hoechst) | Calibrated optical resolution, segmentation mask, circularity index. |
| **Cytoskeletal stress fiber density** | **YES** (Confocal/Actin) | High NA objective (\(\ge 1.3\)), fluorophore staining (e.g. Phalloidin, α-SMA). |
| **Subcellular organelle localization** | **YES** (Confocal/Multicolor) | Colocalization with reference markers (DAPI, Calreticulin, Tubulin). |
| **Secreted cytokine concentration in bath (pg/mL)**| **NO** | Quantitative ELISA, Luminex multiplex bead array, or mass spectrometry. |
| **Kinase phosphorylation (e.g. phospho-Akt Ser473)**| **NO** (in label-free/brightfield) | Phospho-specific immunofluorescence or Western blot with verified antibodies. |
| **Direct physical ligand-receptor binding affinity (\(K_d\))**| **NO** | Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR), Isothermal Titration Calorimetry (ITC), or radioligand binding. |
| **Gene transcription rate (mRNA copies/hour)** | **NO** (without smFISH) | Quantitative RT-PCR, RNA-seq, or single-molecule FISH. |
| **In vivo metastatic organ colonisation** | **NO** (from 2D/3D in vitro) | In vivo xenograft animal model, bioluminescent imaging, histology of lung/liver. |
| **Patient Overall Survival (OS) or Drug Efficacy** | **NO** | Prospective randomized Phase II/III clinical trials with clinical survival endpoints. |

## 3. The 4 Non-Negotiable Anti-Hallucination Guardrails

### Guardrail 1: The "Why Does BioLayers Believe This?" Principle
Every arrow and relation in the BioLayers graph (e.g. `CAF` \(\rightarrow\) `produces` \(\rightarrow\) `CXCL12` \(\rightarrow\) `activates` \(\rightarrow\) `CXCR4` \(\rightarrow\) `mediates` \(\rightarrow\) `cell migration` \(\rightarrow\) `observed in` \(\rightarrow\) `microscopy`) MUST be backed by:
- A specific peer-reviewed publication with verified **PMID** or **DOI**.
- Direct citation quote indicating the biological system and experimental model.
- A verified accession ID (e.g., `BBBC039v1`, `S-BIAD823`, `CTC-Fluo-N2DL-HeLa`).
If an edge does not have empirical or literature support, it **cannot be committed to the database**.

### Guardrail 2: Prohibition of Synthetic Microscopy
No AI-generated (e.g. GAN, Stable Diffusion, DALL-E) microscopy images are allowed in the primary scientific database. All images must originate from physical microscopes operated by verified research laboratories with logged optics, objectives, pixel sizes, and experimental protocols.

### Guardrail 3: Transparent Negative Boundaries (Question 7 in Curation)
In every dataset curation card, Section 7 must explicitly list **"Что нельзя вывести из данных"** (What cannot be deduced). If a model predicts that a drug cures a tumor based on nuclear rounding in vitro, the system must trigger a guardrail flag stating:
> *"Caution: Nuclear rounding reflects cellular stress/apoptosis in vitro; it does not indicate in vivo tumor regression or clinical remission."*

### Guardrail 4: Modality Calibration Guard
No quantitative spatial measurements (µm, µm²) may be displayed if the metadata field `pixel_size` is missing or uncalibrated. When pixel size is uncalibrated, metrics must be displayed strictly in pixel units (`px`, `px²`) with a prominent warning flag.
